## Research and competitive source notes

- Mobile and short landscape: stacked scenes, small character movement, no pinning.
- Reduced motion: complete static scenes; no kinetic masking or draft overlay over the final interface.
- Added sequences: logo mask reveal, IA path drawing, explanatory wireframe-to-actual-UI reveal, review comparison alignment and environment layers. All scrubbed sequences reverse with scrolling.
- The progress readout describes the exhibition, not a live countdown or a changing estimate. The frozen app fixture remains unchanged.
